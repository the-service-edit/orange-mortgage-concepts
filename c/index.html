01First home

First-time buyers often need more explanation. Laszlo will work through your deposit, lending options and what the bank needs before you apply.

02Your next home

Moving to a different home can change what you need from a loan. Discuss the finance, structure and timing before you commit.

03Refinancing

Orange can ask your existing bank to improve its offer, then compare other lenders and the costs of switching.

04Property investment

Discuss investment-property finance and compare loan products and structures against your circumstances.

05Building a home

Construction lenders have specific requirements. Orange helps you prepare the information the bank needs and manage the loan application.

Orange also assists with SMSF lending, parental guarantees and finance for new arrivals to Australia.

03REVIEWING YOUR LOAN

When did you last
review your loan?

Orange aims to contact clients every 12 months to check for changes in their circumstances. Every two or three years, it reviews their loan profile and looks for a better rate with their existing lender or another bank. A review may also find that staying put makes sense.
